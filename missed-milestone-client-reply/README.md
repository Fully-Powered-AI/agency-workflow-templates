# Missed milestone client reply

When a client challenges a delay, a deliverable or an earlier commitment, the account director gets a draft reply and an internal action note that keep confirmed facts apart from proposed recovery steps and the decisions still needed. Nothing is sent.

*Workflow template · Marketing and advertising · Customer success and account management · version 0.1.3 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/missed-milestone-client-reply/)*

## The outcome

The account director answers an unhappy client with the record in hand instead of from memory. The reply responds to what the client actually said, states only what the project records and the delivery team support, and leaves every decision that belongs to the director visibly open. The client gets a prompt, specific answer, and the agency has not promised a date, a credit or a position on who was at fault before anyone with the authority has chosen.

The same pass produces the internal note the director needs before sending: which of the client's claims the records support, which they contradict, which are not in the records at all, and what has to be decided today.

## Who starts the work, and when

The account director, or the account manager on the director's behalf, starts it when a client message challenges a missed date, an unsent deliverable or something the agency said earlier. It runs once per challenge. The director reads both pieces, edits them, takes the delivery team's confirmations, and decides whether, when and how the reply goes out. The director also decides on any concession and on any revised commitment.

## What they supply

- **The client's message,** word for word, with the date it arrived.
- **The agreed milestones:** what was due, on which date, where it was agreed and who owns it.
- **The approval history:** each submission and each response, with dates.
- **The scope and terms notes:** the lines of the statement of work or schedule about revision rounds, feedback windows and extras.
- **The record of what was said:** anything either side told the other about scope, dates or extras, and where.
- **Project status from the delivery team,** with any date the delivery lead has confirmed, who confirmed it and when.
- **The recovery options already authorised,** and who authorised them. If there are none, say so.
- **Your reply settings:** sender, tone, channel, working days and how they are counted, any public holiday dates, and who authorises credits and fee changes.
- **The client's subject line and the contact's name,** if you want them used.
- **Today's date.**

## What comes out

1. **A draft reply** to the client, in your style. Facts appear only where a record supports them. Any date, concession or position that nobody has yet approved appears as a marked gap for the director to fill or remove.
2. **An internal action note** with the client's points checked against the records, a dated timeline, the confirmed facts, the proposed recovery steps, the decisions needed with an owner for each, and what stays open.

If the records cannot support a reply, for example when the milestone record is missing, the workflow template says what it needs and drafts nothing.

## What has to change for your company

Name who may approve the wording, any concession and a new date; the workflow template treats all three as the director's. List which recovery options you have already authorised and under what conditions, and keep that list current, because the workflow template will not propose anything outside it. Set your reply style and the channel. Decide where the scope notes and the record of earlier statements live so they can be supplied quickly. Agree how a disputed question of responsibility is handled in your firm; the workflow template leaves it open and flags it.
