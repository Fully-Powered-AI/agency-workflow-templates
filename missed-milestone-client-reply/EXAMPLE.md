# Missed milestone client reply: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The agency, the client, the project, the dates, the quotations and the roles below are all invented. The agency is an invented 28-person marketing agency building a landing page for a client called Client K. First come the inputs the account director supplied, in full, then the draft reply and internal action note the capability returned. The last input is today's date.

## The inputs

**Client message** (received Friday 16 October 2026, 09:10, from Client K's marketing director)

```
We were promised the final landing-page copy on 9 October and nothing has
arrived. Our campaign launches on 26 October and we have lost a week. On
the kickoff call we were told a second round of revisions was included,
and now your team says it is extra. I need a firm build date today, and I
expect this sorted at no cost.
```

**Milestone record**

```

M2 | Landing-page copy, final | 2026-10-09 | SOW v2, schedule table | Copy lead
M3 | Landing-page build, ready for review | 2026-10-21 | SOW v2, schedule table | Web lead
```

**Scope and terms notes**

**Reply settings:** sender the account director; email; plain, warm and direct; Monday to Friday; working days counted after the day of the event; no public holiday dates supplied; the person who authorises credits and fee changes is not named.

**Today's date:** Friday 16 October 2026.

## The draft reply and internal action note

```
DRAFT REPLY: not sent

To: Client K's marketing director
Channel: email
Subject: Landing-page copy: where it stands

Thank you for writing this morning. I understand your campaign launches
on 26 October.

Where the copy stands: version 2 went to you on 8 October. You sent
changes on 12 October, and version 3 went to you on 14 October. It is with
you now for approval.

On the second round of revisions: [DECISION 1: the account director
states the agency's position here.]

Where the build stands: our web lead confirmed on 15 October that the
build takes four working days from the day the copy is approved.
[DECISION 2: the build date, stated only once the web lead has confirmed
it as a date.]

What we can do to help: if you approve the copy by Monday 19 October, our
delivery director has agreed to move your build ahead of another project;
this does not shorten the four-day build. I can also set up a call between
you and our copywriter to settle any remaining changes, arranged within
one working day of your asking. To keep the build on the shortest path, we
need your response on version 3 by today, Friday 16 October, which is the
end of the two-working-day feedback window in section 4 of our statement
of work.

On cost: [DECISION 3: the account director states the agency's position
here, after whoever authorises credits and fee changes has decided.]

[DECISION 4: when the account director will next be in touch.]

[Account director]

---

INTERNAL ACTION NOTE: Client K, landing page, 16 October 2026

Client's points checked
1. "We were promised the final landing-page copy on 9 October": Supported.
   Milestone M2, final copy, agreed 9 October (SOW v2, schedule table).
2. "nothing has arrived": Contradicted. Approval history: version 2
   submitted 8 October; version 3 submitted 14 October. The records do not
   say either was submitted as the final copy, and show no approval.
3. "Our campaign launches on 26 October": Not in the records. The client's
   statement only.
4. "we have lost a week": Responsibility disputed. 9 to 16 October is 7
   calendar days and 5 working days, and the approval history shows no
   approval of the final copy.
   Dated facts: version 2 submitted 8 October; changes requested 12
   October, 2 working days later (the feedback window in SOW section 4);
   version 3 submitted 14 October, 2 working days after the changes were
   requested. Cause not assigned.
5. "we were told a second round of revisions was included, and now your
   team says it is extra": Records disagree. SOW section 3: "Two rounds of
   client revisions on copy are included; further rounds are quoted."
   Kickoff notes, 21 September: "Two rounds of copy revisions included."
   Account manager email, 13 October: "Round 2 is outside the original
   scope and will be quoted." Round 1 was requested 6 October, round 2 on
   12 October. Not resolved here.
6. "I need a firm build date today": Request. See Decision 2. No build
   date is delivery-confirmed.
7. "I expect this sorted at no cost": Request. See Decision 3. No credit,
   fee change or free work is authorised in the options supplied.

Timeline
21 Sep (Mon) Kickoff: two revision rounds included (statements record).
2 Oct (Fri) M1 first draft due; submitted (milestone record; approval
  history). Elapsed since 21 Sep: 11 calendar days; 9 working days
  (22 to 25 Sep, 28 Sep to 2 Oct).
6 Oct (Tue) Client requested changes, round 1 (approval history). Elapsed
  since 2 Oct: 4 calendar days; 2 working days (5, 6 Oct).
8 Oct (Thu) Version 2 submitted (approval history). Elapsed since 6 Oct:
  2 calendar days; 2 working days (7, 8 Oct).
9 Oct (Fri) M2 final copy date (milestone record). Elapsed since 8 Oct:
  1 calendar day; 1 working day (9 Oct).
12 Oct (Mon) Client requested changes, round 2 (approval history).
  Elapsed since 9 Oct: 3 calendar days; 1 working day (12 Oct). Since
  version 2 (8 Oct): 4 calendar days; 2 working days (9, 12 Oct), the
  feedback window in SOW section 4 (scope notes).
13 Oct (Tue) Account manager email: round 2 outside scope (statements
  record). Elapsed since 12 Oct: 1 calendar day; 1 working day (13 Oct).
14 Oct (Wed) Version 3 submitted (approval history). Elapsed since
  13 Oct: 1 calendar day; 1 working day (14 Oct). Since 12 Oct: 2 calendar
  days; 2 working days (13, 14 Oct).
15 Oct (Thu) Web lead confirmed four working days' build from approval
  (delivery status). Delivery director authorised moving the build ahead
  if copy is approved by 19 October (authorised recovery options).
  Elapsed since 14 Oct: 1 calendar day; 1 working day (15 Oct).
16 Oct (Fri) Client message received; version 3 is awaiting approval
  (client message; delivery status). Elapsed since 15 Oct: 1 calendar
  day; 1 working day (16 Oct). Since version 3 (14 Oct): 2 calendar days;
  2 working days (15, 16 Oct), so the SOW feedback window ends today
  (scope notes). Since M2 (9 Oct): 7 calendar days; 5 working days
  (12 to 16 Oct).

Confirmed facts
- Final copy date agreed: 9 October (M2) (milestone record).
- Version 2 submitted 8 October; version 3 submitted 14 October
  (approval history).
- Client requested changes on 6 and 12 October (approval history).
- Version 3 is with the client for approval (delivery status).
- Web lead confirmed on 15 October that the build takes four working days
  from the day copy is approved. No build date confirmed (delivery
  status).
- SOW section 4: client feedback is due within two working days of each
  submission; for version 3 that is 16 October (scope notes).

Proposed recovery steps
- Move the build ahead of another project (delivery director, 15 October;
  conditions: copy approved by Monday 19 October, not yet met; date not
  confirmed).
- Joint call with the copywriter (account director; conditions: client
  asks for the call and the agency arranges it within one working day of
  the request; date not confirmed).

Decisions needed
- DECISION 1: the agency's position on round 2. Decides: account director
  (and whoever handles contracts, if it becomes a question about the
  SOW's meaning). Facts: point 5. Needed by: no deadline stated by the
  client.
- DECISION 2: whether and what build date to state, and the revised
  commitment on M3 (agreed 21 October). Decides: account director, after
  the web lead confirms a date. Facts: only a duration is confirmed; the
  arithmetic from a confirmed duration, not a confirmed date: copy
  approved Friday 16 October gives Thursday 22 October, already after
  21 October; approved Monday 19 October gives Friday 23 October; approved
  Tuesday 20 October gives Monday 26 October, the launch day; approved
  Wednesday 21 October gives Tuesday 27 October, after it. Needed by:
  today, per the client.
- DECISION 3: the agency's position on the client's expectation of no
  cost. Decides: account director; any credit, fee change or free work is
  for whoever authorises them, and none is authorised in the options
  supplied. Facts: points 5 and 7. Needed by: no deadline stated on cost;
  the client's "today" attaches to the build date.
- DECISION 4: when the account director will next be in touch. Decides:
  account director. Facts: none in the records. Needed by: before sending.

Left open
- Responsibility for the week since 9 October is not assigned.
- Which of SOW section 3 and the 13 October email governs round 2 is not
  settled. If it becomes a question of contract terms, take it to whoever
  handles contracts.
- The 26 October launch date has not been checked against any project
  record.
- No public holiday dates were supplied, so every Monday to Friday was
  counted as a working day.
- Whether feedback on version 3 would count as a third round, which the
  SOW says is quoted, is not settled; the request for a response could be
  read as inviting one.
- The person who authorises credits and fee changes is not named in the
  inputs.

Before sending
- The web lead confirms whether any build date can be stated (Decision 2).
- The delivery director's 15 October agreement is still in force.
- The reply states 16 October, the end of the SOW section 4 feedback
  window, as the date for the client's response. That deadline falls on
  the day of sending; decide whether to state it today, given that
  approval by Monday 19 October is what the build-ahead option needs.
- The joint call is read as arranged by the agency within one working day
  of the client asking; the option's conditions say so.
- The subject line is the assistant's wording and the contact's name was
  not supplied; check both.
- Confirm that version 3 reached the right contact on 14 October; the
  approval history records the submission, not receipt.
- Fill in or remove each of Decisions 1 to 4.

Nothing has been sent, offered or committed. The account director approves
the reply, any concession and any revised commitment.
```
