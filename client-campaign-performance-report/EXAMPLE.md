# Client campaign performance report: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* An invented agency prepares the weekly report for Client A, an invented online shop selling outdoor furniture. The agency, the client, the platforms and every figure below are made up for this example; none of them stands for a real organisation.

## What the analyst supplies

**Run settings.** Reporting window Monday 2 November to Sunday 8 November 2026 (7 days). Cutoff Monday 9 November. Previous period Monday 26 October to Sunday 1 November (7 days), taken from last week's report. Currency USD. The agency's format is the template's default.

**Definitions.** Search platform ("Platform A"): conversions are completed purchases, 30-day click window. Social platform ("Platform B"): conversions are completed purchases, 7-day click and 1-day view window. Analytics tool: transactions credited to the last non-direct click source. Ratios are built from summed figures.

**Client goals.** Search return on ad spend of at least 8.00 (Platform A's figures). Social cost per conversion of at most $15.00 (Platform B's figures). Total ad spend for the week within $1,500.00 across both platforms.

**Notes from the media buyer.** (1) Platform B's conversion tracking was down on 4 and 5 November and fixed on 6 November. (2) The saved date range on the Platform A export ended on 7 November. (3) Nothing else was changed in either account this week.

**Platform A export** (columns: Date, Campaign, Spend, Impressions, Clicks, Conversions, Conversion value). One campaign, "Search - Outdoor Sets". Six rows; there is no row for 8 November.
